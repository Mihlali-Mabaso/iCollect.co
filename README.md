# iCollect Logistics 🚚

**iCollect Logistics** is a tech-driven, last-mile courier and delivery tracking platform launching in **Bloemfontein, Free State**, with plans for expansion across South Africa. 

Built on the core philosophy of **"Quiet Logistics"**, iCollect eliminates delivery anxiety for businesses and individuals through real-time telemetry, proactive driver updates, and white-glove parcel handling.

---

## 🌟 Company Overview & Value Proposition

### 1. **Quiet Logistics & Proactive Updates**
Traditional delivery often leaves recipients in a "black hole" of silence, resulting in constant *"Where is my order?"* inquiries to merchants. 

iCollect automates real-time tracking links and **WhatsApp / SMS notifications** directly to end-recipients. If a delay occurs due to weather or traffic, drivers send a proactive update within **5 minutes** explaining the delay and updated delivery window.

### 2. **Two Market Focus Areas**
- **For Businesses (SMEs, E-Commerce Stores, Retailers):** Monthly delivery subscription tiers (Starter, Growth, Enterprise), automated branded customer notifications, driver dispatch dashboard, and a checkout partner badge (*"Delivered by iCollect"*).
- **For Individuals:** Fast, on-demand local delivery booking in under 60 seconds with simple pay-per-delivery rates.

---

## 💰 Service Tiers & Pricing Structure

### **Individual Delivery Tiers**
- **Local Drop (R49):** Within 5 km radius for light parcels up to 5 kg.
- **City Delivery (R89):** Standard 5–20 km delivery across Bloemfontein up to 10 kg.
- **Express (R149):** Priority dispatch, same-day delivery up to 20 kg.

### **Business Subscription Tiers**
- **Starter (R799 / month):** Up to 20 deliveries/month + tracking portal.
- **Growth (R1 899 / month):** Up to 60 deliveries/month + branded notifications.
- **Enterprise (Custom):** 100+ monthly deliveries, dedicated account management, and SLA guarantee.
