Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. This policy explains how iCollect Logistics collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

01

Who We Are

iCollect Logistics ("iCollect", "we", "us", or "our") is a courier and delivery tracking platform registered and operating in Bloemfontein, South Africa. We provide delivery management services to businesses and individual customers.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via WhatsApp at our support channel or through the contact form.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, and information generated through your use of our services.

Information You Provide

  • — Full name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • — Pickup and delivery addresses
  • — Parcel descriptions and special handling instructions
  • — Business name and registration details (for business accounts)
  • — Payment and billing information

Information Collected Automatically

  • — Delivery tracking data and GPS location (during active deliveries)
  • — Device and browser information when accessing our platform
  • — Usage patterns and interaction logs
  • — IP address and approximate geographic location

03

How We Use Your Information

  • — To process and fulfil your delivery requests
  • — To provide real-time tracking updates via WhatsApp, SMS, or in-app notifications
  • — To assign drivers and manage our logistics operations
  • — To communicate with you about your orders and account
  • — To improve our services and develop new features
  • — To comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes
  • — To send service-related announcements (you may opt out at any time)

04

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • — Drivers and delivery partners — only the information necessary to complete your delivery (name, contact number, delivery address).
  • — Business clients — if you are a recipient of a business's shipment, relevant tracking information is shared with that business.
  • — Service providers — third parties who help us operate our platform (e.g., payment processors, SMS/WhatsApp notification services), bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • — Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of iCollect, our clients, or the public.

05

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with our legal obligations. Delivery records are typically retained for a minimum of 12 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to our legal retention requirements.

06

Your Rights

Under applicable South African law (POPIA — Protection of Personal Information Act), you have the right to:

  • — Access the personal information we hold about you
  • — Request correction of inaccurate information
  • — Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • — Object to the processing of your information
  • — Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise these rights, contact us via our contact form.

07

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions about your privacy?

We are happy to clarify anything in this policy.
